# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, internship coordinators, and engineering peers arriving from Adam's resume, LinkedIn, Handshake, or GitHub. They give the page 30–60 seconds; their job is to decide "is this person worth an interview / a follow?" Secondary: club members and collaborators checking out projects.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Adam Stepansky (adamstepansky.com) — CS student at Brandeis '28, co-founder of Lodian AI, software engineer intern. The site exists to convert a visit into an interview: show real, shipped projects (several live), current roles, and skills, and make contact effortless. Success = recruiter clicks a live project or the resume and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Technical & distinctive. Precise, engineered, quietly confident — "built, not decorated." Reads like the workbench of someone who ships: terminal-adjacent details (mono annotations, status markers) used with restraint, never costume. Memorable first impression, hireable second impression.

## Anti-references

- Generic student-portfolio template (Bootstrap hero + three identical cards).
- Corporate resume-as-a-webpage stiffness.
- Neon hacker/gamer aesthetic: pulsing glows, matrix-green overload, over-animation.
- Sparse/unfinished minimalism that reads as an empty page.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over polish** — live projects and real roles are the content; the design frames them like a spec sheet, not a brochure.
2. **Terminal accent, not terminal costume** — mono type and status markers season the page; body reading stays humanist and comfortable.
3. **Structure is the style** — hairline rules, aligned grids, and annotation labels carry the aesthetic instead of cards, glows, and gradients.
4. **One good motion moment** — a single orchestrated hero entrance plus precise hover feedback; nothing pulses idly.
5. **30-second legibility** — a recruiter skimming must hit name → role → best projects → contact without hunting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 on the dark ground, focus-visible states on all interactive elements, full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives, semantic landmarks/headings, alt text with voice.
